📊 Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Birth Date: Used only for age verification and determining if parental controls are needed. Never transmitted anywhere.
  • User-Created Content: All milestones, steps, storybooks, and images are stored locally on your device only.

🔒 How We Store Your Information

  • Local Storage: All data is stored securely on your device using iOS Keychain and Core Data with encryption.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not collect, store, or transmit any data to servers — because we don't have any servers!
  • No Third Parties: We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

📸 Photo & Camera Access

We request access to your photo library and camera so you can:

  • Add photos to your milestones and steps
  • Take photos directly within the app
  • Create visual storybooks for your family

Your photos never leave your device.

🎬 YouTube Videos

Steps in the app can include YouTube video links for tutorials or guidance:

  • Videos are played using YouTube's embedded player
  • YouTube's own privacy policy applies during video playback
  • No YouTube data is stored by our app
  • For users under 18, video links require parental approval before being added

👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We take children's privacy very seriously:

  • Age Verification: We require birth date for age verification only — it never leaves your device
  • Parental Controls: For users under 18, we require a separate parent PIN during setup
  • Parent Approval: External links and videos require parent approval for under-18 users
  • No Data Collection: We do not collect any personal information from children (or anyone!)
  • No Third Parties: We never share anyone's data with third parties
  • Safe Mode: Additional privacy controls for sensitive content

🔐 Data Security

  • PIN Protection: PINs are hashed using PBKDF2 with salt (100,000 iterations) and stored in iOS Keychain
  • Biometric Support: Face ID and Touch ID for convenient, secure access
  • Local Encryption: All data is protected by iOS encryption (FileProtection.complete)
  • Rate Limiting: Account lockout after 5 failed login attempts (15-minute cooldown)
  • Secure Storage: All sensitive data uses Apple's secure Keychain technology

🗑️ Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the app — it's YOUR data
  • Delete: Deleting the app permanently removes all data from your device
  • No Server Data: Since we don't store data on servers, there's nothing to request from us

🌍 International Users

This app works entirely offline. Your data never leaves your device, regardless of your location. No data crosses borders because no data goes anywhere!

📱 Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party services including:

  • ❌ No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • ❌ No advertising networks
  • ❌ No crash reporting services
  • ❌ No social media integrations
  • ❌ No cloud storage services
  • ❌ No tracking of any kind
